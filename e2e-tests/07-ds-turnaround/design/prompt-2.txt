- About 14 flat colours in total for the creature. Each material (plum skin, sage-green leaves and fern spirals, cream belly) uses 3 shading steps; the eyes use a dark outline, a bright amber ring and a black pupil with one white highlight pixel.
- Outline: mostly near-black on the outer silhouette, and the darkest shade of each material inside the silhouette where parts overlap.
- Light from the upper left. Shadows that explain the form: the head casts a shadow onto the leaf collar, darker pixels where arms and legs join the body, a darker underside of the tail.
- Plain flat colours, no gradients, no dithering, no blur, no anti-aliasing against the background.
